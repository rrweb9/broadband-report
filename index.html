How this was measured

Full capacity test, 4x a day. Apple's built-in networkQuality tool saturates the connection with about ten parallel streams to a nearby Apple server. This is the honest measure of what the line can do, and it is the only source for the speed figures above.

Quick check, every 10 minutes. Ten pings, a DNS lookup and a few short transfers. Its job is latency, jitter, packet loss and catching dropouts - not speed.

Why the quick check's throughput is not shown as a speed. Short transfers to one provider measured 50-142 Mbps on this line while the full test measured 444-475 Mbps, and enlarging the transfer did not close the gap. That reflects one route rather than the connection, and its cause is unproven, so it is kept out of the headline numbers. It is in the table and the CSV for completeness.
